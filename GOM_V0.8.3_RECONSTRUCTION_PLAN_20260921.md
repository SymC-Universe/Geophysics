## Native-first rebuild sequence

1. Inventory historical claims separately for fault friction, viscoelastic relaxation, attenuation, aseismic creep, poroelasticity, magma/volcanic transport, GNSS/deformation, and coupled lithosphere behavior.
2. Bind every candidate coordinate to a native model and classify it as mechanically licensed chi, empirical/proxy coordinate, alternative normalized coordinate, or refused.
3. Rebuild representative Function Maps before centering rare rupture/eruption cases.
4. Rebuild Limit Maps with post-result selection and promotion debt explicit for rare/extreme natural events.
5. Separate local/modal behavior from fault-zone/region/system Chi architecture and test their relationship rather than averaging local values.
6. For perturbational or event-recovery claims, distinguish resistance, finite-time response, first reclaim, sustained recovery, reorganization, basin robustness, and repeated-event behavior where observable.
7. Separate retrospective event reconstruction from untouched prediction.
8. Benchmark warning/forecast claims against accepted seismological, geodetic, frictional, and volcanological methods.
9. Freeze any new prediction/event definition before the untouched period or system is inspected and complete MFR-14 for P1.

## Stop lines

- no universal Earth/Mars/Venus chi window without independently licensed comparable coordinates;
- no rupture or eruption timing claim from retrospective fit alone;
- no empirical ratio relabeled mechanical damping without a generator;
- no cross-domain numeric recurrence promoted to common mechanism;
- no stability=recovery identity.
